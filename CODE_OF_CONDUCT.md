# Code of conduct

Better ETLab is a small project made by students, for students. We want it to be
a friendly place for everyone, whatever their year, branch, background or
experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to coding or to open source.
- Give feedback about the code, not the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Respect other people's privacy. Never share anyone's data, marks or attendance.

## What is not okay

- Insults, harassment, or personal or discriminatory attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information, including their ETLab data.
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions.

## If something goes wrong

If someone makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer
([@AbhineethVS](https://github.com/AbhineethVS)) privately. The repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form is private too, and is fine to use for
this. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block people who break these
rules.
